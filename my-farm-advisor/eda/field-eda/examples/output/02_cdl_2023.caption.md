# M2 — 2023 crop map

**Question:** What was planted where in the most recent CDL year?

**What this shows:** Corn and soybean blocks separate spatially, Nebraska is almost solid corn, and the Illinois grass outlier stands out immediately. One shared legend is used for every grower so the colours mean the same crop across panels.

**Caveat:** 30 m CDL pixels. The smallest field-year in this run has 4 pixels, so a small parcel's class is a handful of pixels rather than a clean field average.

**Reading notes:**
- Farm overview in local UTM kilometres; no auto-rotation.
